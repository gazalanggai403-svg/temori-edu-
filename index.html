Functional breakfast bar

START THE DAY STRONGER.

TEMORI is a plant-based functional breakfast bar developed as a practical nutritional intervention for adolescents, combining locally available ingredients with protein, iron, and dietary fiber.

Explore TEMORI ↓
01 — Introduction

What is TEMORI?

🥜 Plant-Based

TEMORI is designed using locally sourced plant ingredients to provide a practical and convenient breakfast option.

🩸 Nutritional Focus

The concept focuses on supporting adequate intake of protein, iron, and fiber—nutrients relevant to adolescent nutrition.

🌱 Local Innovation

By prioritizing accessible ingredients, TEMORI demonstrates how local food resources can be developed into functional products.

02 — Why it matters

Nutrition should be practical.

Adolescence is a period of rapid growth. A convenient food product can help make nutritious choices easier to access, especially when busy schedules encourage skipping breakfast.

Key focus

Protein, iron & fiber.

Protein
Supports normal growth and maintenance of body tissues.
Iron
Is an essential micronutrient involved in normal oxygen transport in the body.
Dietary fiber
Contributes to a balanced diet and supports normal digestive function.

Important note

TEMORI is presented as a functional food concept and is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or replace professional medical care for anemia or other nutritional conditions.

Actual nutritional values should be confirmed through laboratory analysis of the final formulation.

03 — From ingredients to bar

How TEMORI is developed

1Ingredient preparation

Selected ingredients are prepared and processed into suitable forms.

2Mixing

Ingredients are combined according to the intended formulation.

3Molding & baking

The mixture is shaped into bars and processed into the final product.

4Cooling & packaging

Bars are cooled before packaging and evaluation.
